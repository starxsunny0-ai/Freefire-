FAQ

Questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Installing and using Fraglord is free. Each match has its own entry fee, which is shown before you join.

Is it safe to install?

Fraglord is a light app that mainly needs internet access. Android may show a standard warning because the file comes from outside the Play Store. Choose “Install anyway” to continue.

How do I add money?

Open Wallet, tap Add Money, enter an amount between ₹10 and ₹10,000 and pay with any UPI app. The wallet updates by itself.

How do withdrawals work?

Request a withdrawal from your Winnings (minimum ₹100). The admin team checks it and pays your UPI ID. You can follow the status in your wallet.

What if a match gets cancelled?

Your entry fee is refunded to your wallet automatically when a match is cancelled or does not fill the minimum players.

Where do I find the Room ID?

Join a match, then open it. The Room ID and password appear a few minutes before the start time.

The app is not working

Close and open it again. If the problem stays, message support with your username and a screenshot.
